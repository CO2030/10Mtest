About us

Less admin, more time with patients.

Built to lighten the load on dentists, by a practicing dentist.

Dentists work long days, and much of the stress comes from paperwork, claims, and scheduling. Mitsuha takes that work off the desk, so you can focus on your patients and your team.

Our mission

We build dental software that makes running a practice simpler, so dentists and their teams spend less time on admin and more time with patients.

Our vision

Every dental office runs on software that carries the busywork, so dentists and their teams go home without burnout.
