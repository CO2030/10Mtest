Archy alternative: how Mitsuha compares

Mitsuha is cloud dental practice management software, like Archy, with AI Scribe, AI insurance verification and Amy, an AI phone assistant, built in. Here is a fair look at where each one fits.

How are Mitsuha and Archy alike?

Both are cloud-based, all-in-one dental practice management systems. Both cover scheduling, charting, imaging, claims, eligibility checks and patient tools, and both run in a web browser with no office server.

How are Mitsuha and Archy different?

Mitsuha is built by dentists, with multiple offices that run on Mitsuha every day, and it includes AI Scribe, AI insurance verification and Amy in the core product. Archy publishes a flat platform price and sells its AI clinical and front-office tools as separate suites. Archy's platform includes text-to-pay, a patient wallet and email marketing, which Mitsuha doesn't offer today.

Which practices are a better fit for Mitsuha?

Practices that want AI billing, AI insurance verification, AI Scribe and an AI phone assistant without adding separate suites tend to fit Mitsuha best. Practices that need text-to-pay, a patient wallet or e-prescribing today may find Archy fits better for now.
