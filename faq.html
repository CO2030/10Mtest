Questions dentists ask us

Dental software FAQ: cloud dental software questions, answered

Mitsuha is cloud dental practice management software, and these are straight answers to the questions dentists ask us most. They cover cost, switching, security, AI billing and training, from the dentists and office managers who use Mitsuha every day.

Dental software cost

How much does dental practice management software cost?

You can get a custom pricing quote by booking a 20-minute demo right on this page. Simply share a few quick details about your dental practice—including your number of locations, active providers, and current practice management software—and select a time that works for your team.

During the call, we will walk you through a personalized workflow tailored to a practice like yours and provide transparent, all-in pricing covering your monthly subscription, data migration, and onboarding.

Switching dental software

How long does it take to switch dental software?

Most dental practices go live within 3 to 4 weeks after signing, with the majority of that timeline driven by standard electronic insurance billing and clearinghouse paperwork.

During your onboarding window, our implementation team locks in your target go-live date, audits and migrates your patient data, and delivers role-based training for your front desk, hygienists, assistants, and doctors—ensuring your entire team feels confident before day one.

What data can I bring over from my current software?

What transfers into Mitsuha depends on whether you are opening a new dental practice or migrating from an existing practice management system—and our onboarding team reviews your exact database with you step by step before you switch:

  • New dental startups: If you are launching a brand-new office with no legacy database, setup is fast and straightforward—we configure your schedules, fee schedules, and digital intake forms from scratch.
  • Established practices switching software: Before your go-live date, we audit your current system together and map out a clear conversion plan. Depending on your existing software, migration typically covers core records such as patient demographics, clinical charts, digital X-rays, insurance details, and account ledgers.

Because every legacy database is structured differently, you will receive a transparent breakdown of exactly what moves over—and how historical records are handled—well before your go-live date.

Does Mitsuha work with my imaging sensors?

Yes. Mitsuha includes built-in, native dental imaging software, eliminating the need to license or bridge a separate third-party imaging program. It connects directly to intraoral X-ray sensors and panoramic X-ray machines that support standard TWAIN drivers, saving captured images straight into the patient’s digital chart and corresponding tooth.

Because certain hardware models require minor driver configuration, our implementation team audits and tests your exact imaging setup with you prior to go-live.

Dental software security and HIPAA

Is cloud dental software HIPAA compliant?

Yes—cloud-based dental practice management software is HIPAA compliant when the vendor implements required administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) and executes a formal Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with the dental practice.

Mitsuha meets strict HIPAA compliance standards—using encrypted data transmission, secure cloud infrastructure, and role-based user permissions—and signs a BAA with every dental practice before onboarding.

Do I need a server in my office?

No on-premise server is required. Because Mitsuha is cloud-based dental practice management software, your practice avoids the upfront hardware costs, local server backups, and ongoing IT maintenance tied to traditional server-based systems. Your team logs in directly through a web browser on your existing office computers to access real-time schedules, clinical charts, and billing across any location—without VPN lag—while new features and security updates arrive automatically without downtime.

AI dental billing

What is AI dental billing?

AI dental billing is practice management software that automates repetitive dental insurance and revenue cycle workflows—including real-time insurance eligibility verification, building clean claims directly from completed CDT procedures, electronic claim submission, ERA payment posting, and automated follow-up on unpaid or aging claims.

Instead of manually chasing payers for every visit, Mitsuha’s AI dental billing handles routine claim processing in the background and flags only the exceptions that genuinely require human review—helping practices accelerate reimbursements and reduce claim denials. Explore the full workflow on our AI Dental Billing page.

Dental software training and support

What training and support do you provide—from onboarding onward?

Mitsuha combines hands-on onboarding with built-in in-app guidance and ongoing support so your team is supported before launch, on day one, and as you grow:

  • Role-based pre-go-live onboarding: Before you switch, our team walks each role in your office—front desk, billing, hygiene, assistants, and doctors—through tailored training sessions so everyone knows their daily workflows before launch.
  • On-demand guidance with Ask Megumi: Inside the software, Ask Megumi gives your staff and future new hires instant, plain-language answers and interactive step-by-step walkthroughs across clinical charting, scheduling, imaging, and billing—without pulling senior staff away from patients.
  • Ongoing support & in-app chat: After go-live, your team can reach us anytime through our built-in support and feedback chat right inside Mitsuha.
