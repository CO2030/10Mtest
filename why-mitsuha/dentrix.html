Dentrix alternative

A modern Dentrix alternative, built by dentists

Mitsuha is a cloud Dentrix alternative with AI billing, built by dentists who run their own offices on it. Dentrix runs on a server in your office, with electronic claims and eligibility checks as add-ons. Mitsuha runs in your browser with both built in.

How does Mitsuha compare with Dentrix?

Dentrix is a traditional, server-based system, and many of the tools practices now rely on cost extra. With Mitsuha, they're part of the software.

Compared with Dentrix (server-based), not Dentrix Ascend.

Why choose software built by dentists, for dentists?

Because multiple offices run on Mitsuha every day, every feature is tested in a real practice before it reaches yours. Mitsuha wasn't built by a software company guessing at dental work.

We lived it

Multiple offices run on Mitsuha. The missed claims, insurance calls and late-night billing were our problems first.

We tested it

Every feature runs in our own operatories and front desks before it reaches yours.

We know what you need

AI billing, insurance checks, patient calls and imaging come included, not as add-ons. When you need help, you talk to people who know dental offices from the inside.

What data moves over from Dentrix?

We review your Dentrix data with you before you switch, so you know what comes over. These are the records we focus on.

Patient charts

Conditions, treatment history and notes.

X-rays and images

Your imaging history [confirm].

Insurance

Plans, subscribers and coverage details.

Ledgers

Balances and payment history.

How long does it take to switch from Dentrix?

From signing to live in about 3 to 4 weeks. Most of that time is insurance billing paperwork. Here is what happens along the way.

  1. 1

    Plan

    We review your Dentrix setup and pick a go-live date.

  2. 2

    Move your data

    We copy your Dentrix data and check it with you.

  3. 3

    Train your team

    Each role gets training for their daily work.

  4. 4

    Go live

    We stay close for the first weeks.
