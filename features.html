Everything in one place

All-in-one dental practice management software: everything your practice runs on

Mitsuha is cloud dental software that puts scheduling, charting, AI Scribe, X-rays, AI billing, insurance verification, a patient app, Amy and reports in one system. Your team logs in from any browser, at any office.

AI billing

Scheduling

Dental scheduling software

Mitsuha shows every chair, provider and hygienist for the day in one view, with insurance status right on each appointment.

  • Multi-office and multi-provider views
  • Coverage status on each appointment
  • Open-slot view for quick bookings

Charting

Dental charting and treatment planning

Mitsuha gives you a clear tooth chart with existing work and planned treatment, plus treatment plans with insurance estimates.

  • Tooth chart with conditions and planned work
  • Treatment plans with insurance estimates
  • Clinical notes in the same record

AI Scribe

What does AI Scribe do during a dental visit?

AI Scribe writes the clinical notes, adds the procedure codes and builds the treatment plan while the dentist talks through the exam as usual. It then sends everything on to billing, ready to go.

  • SOAP notes, perio charting and existing conditions by voice
  • Codes and treatment plans filled in from the visit
  • Rendered services flow straight to billing and claims
  • Coding errors flagged before a claim is created

Imaging

Dental imaging software built into the chart

Mitsuha has its own imaging software, built in-house, so you don't need a separate imaging program. It connects to intraoral sensors and panoramic machines that use standard TWAIN drivers, and bitewings, periapicals and panos open in the patient's record, linked to the tooth they belong to. Adjust brightness and contrast without leaving the chart.

  • Our own imaging software, no separate program to buy
  • Connects to TWAIN intraoral sensors and pano machines
  • Images linked to teeth on the chart
  • Brightness, contrast and zoom tools
  • Attach images to claims in one click

Patient app

What can patients do in the Mitsuha patient app?

Patients can book, reschedule or cancel visits, update their details, manage family members, check their coverage and pay their bills from their phone. Everything they do shows up in Mitsuha right away, so your front desk takes fewer calls.

  • Book, reschedule or cancel appointments
  • Update their own details
  • Manage family members and their visits
  • Check their insurance coverage
  • Pay their bills online
  • Get appointment reminders

Amy · AI assistant

What is Amy, the AI phone assistant?

Amy is Mitsuha's AI assistant that makes routine patient calls: confirmations, rescheduling, recalls, filling open slots and preauthorization updates. Every outcome lands on the schedule and in the patient record.

  • Confirms and reschedules on the spot
  • Recall calls for overdue patients
  • Calls when a preauthorization is approved or denied
  • Call notes saved automatically
