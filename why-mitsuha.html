Why Mitsuha: switching to cloud dental software

Mitsuha is cloud dental software that replaces the server in your office, adds AI billing and runs every office in one system. Practices on server-based software switch to Mitsuha to drop the office server, get AI billing, and use one system across every office. We handle the move, including charts, X-rays, insurance and ledgers.

What changes when you move from a server to cloud dental software?

With cloud dental software there's no office server to buy or back up, updates arrive automatically, and every office shares one system.

Server in your office

  • A server to buy, back up and replace
  • Updates installed on each computer
  • Remote access set up separately
  • Each office often runs its own copy

Mitsuha in the cloud

  • No office server to maintain
  • Updates arrive automatically
  • Log in from any browser you approve
  • One system across all your offices

How does Mitsuha compare with Dentrix, Eaglesoft and Open Dental?

Mitsuha runs in the cloud with AI billing built in, while Dentrix, Eaglesoft and Open Dental are traditionally installed on a server in your office. Each comparison shows features side by side, what moves over and how long switching takes.

How does switching to Mitsuha work?

Our team plans your go-live date, moves your data and trains your staff, and practices usually go live in about 3 to 4 weeks. We moved multiple offices onto Mitsuha, so we know what a switch takes.

  1. 1

    Plan

    We review your current system and pick a go-live date that avoids your busiest weeks.

  2. 2

    Move your data

    We review your current data with you, then bring over charts, X-rays, insurance and ledgers.

  3. 3

    Train your team

    Front desk, billing and clinical staff each get training for their own work.

  4. 4

    Go live

    We stay close for the first weeks and answer questions as they come up.
