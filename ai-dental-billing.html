Billing that does itself

Dental insurance verification software with AI billing built in

Mitsuha is dental insurance verification software with AI billing: it checks coverage before each visit, then builds, sends and follows up on your claims. Your team only steps in on the claims that need a person.

How does AI dental billing work?

Mitsuha checks coverage, builds the claim from the chart, sends it and follows up until it's paid. Here are the four steps.

Steps to confirm with the product team [confirm]

  1. 1

    Checks coverage

    Before each visit, eligibility and benefits are checked for the patient's plan.

  2. 2

    Builds the claim

    When the visit is complete, the claim is built from the procedures in the chart.

  3. 3

    Sends and tracks

    Claims go out electronically, and every status update is tracked in one list.

  4. 4

    Follows up

    Unpaid or denied claims are followed up, and anything unusual is flagged for your team.

How does AI insurance verification work before each visit?

Mitsuha checks each patient's eligibility and benefits automatically before the appointment and shows the results on the schedule, so there are fewer surprises at checkout. Your team can also check a whole day or week in one click.

  • Plan status, deductible and remaining maximum
  • Coverage by category: preventive, basic and major
  • Flags for plans that need a closer look

How much time does dental claims management take with AI billing?

AI billing takes over the benefit checks, claim building and status tracking that billing coordinators now do by hand. Here is the daily work of billing out claims and pre-auths, before and after, in the offices that run on Mitsuha.

Before: claims by hand

3–4 hrs a day

  • Calling payers to check benefits
  • Re-typing procedures into claim forms
  • Checking portals for claim status
  • Chasing denials one by one

After: with Mitsuha AI billing

1–2 hrs a day

  • Benefits checked automatically
  • Claims built from the chart
  • Status tracked in one list
  • Only flagged claims need a person
